Privacy Policy

AF Master Project Tracker (afmpt) · Last updated: 2026-04-29

Who runs this app

The AF Master Project Tracker (“AFMPT”, “the app”) is an internal tool operated by SmartOSC for use by employees of the @smartosc.com Google Workspace. It is not offered to the general public.

What data we collect

  • Google Workspace identity: your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and Firebase Auth user ID. We collect this when you sign in with Google.
  • Project content you create: program/workstream/task names, owners, dates, deliverables, dependencies, and weekly check-in notes you post.
  • Activity log: a trail of who edited what (capped at the most recent 500 entries per project). Each entry stores your verified email as the author.
  • Per-user preferences: the project ID you last viewed.

What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising tags.
  • No location data, device fingerprinting, or browsing history outside the app.
  • No access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or any other Google service.

How we use your data

  • Authenticate you and confirm you belong to @smartosc.com.
  • Display your name and photo on changes and check-ins for team attribution.
  • Keep an audit-style log of edits so the team can see who did what.

Where your data lives

Project content and your profile metadata are stored in Google Firestore (Firebase) in the asia-southeast1 region. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Static assets are served by Vercel. We do not transfer your data to any other third party.

Access controls

The app enforces three layers of access control:

  • Sign-in is restricted to verified @smartosc.com Google identities.
  • Each project has a member list and per-user roles (Owner / Operator / Viewer). Only members of a project can read its content; non-members are blocked at the database layer (Firestore security rules).
  • Activity-log entries are signed with your verified email server-side — teammates cannot post entries that pretend to be from someone else.

Retention

Project content remains in Firestore until a project owner deletes it. Deleted projects are soft-deleted (hidden but recoverable from the Firebase console). The activity log is capped at 500 entries per project and rotates oldest-first.

Your rights

  • Access: every project member can already see the project's content, including check-ins they posted. You can export any project as JSON or Excel from the app toolbar.
  • Correction: edit your own check-ins or task fields directly. To change your display name or photo, update your Google Workspace profile.
  • Deletion: ask a workspace admin (see contact below) to remove your check-ins and directory entry. Project content you authored may remain if other members rely on it; the admin will discuss case-by-case.

Cookies & local storage

The app uses Firebase Authentication's secure session storage to remember that you are signed in. We do not set marketing or tracking cookies.

Children

The app is for SmartOSC employees only and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced in the team channel before they take effect. The “Last updated” date above shows the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or data handling: contact the AF team workspace admin at ai-bo-02@smartosc.com.
