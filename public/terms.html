Access to the AF Master Project Tracker (“AFMPT”) is restricted to current employees of SmartOSC who hold an active @smartosc.com Google Workspace identity. By signing in you confirm that you are such an employee.

2. Acceptable use

  • Use the app only for SmartOSC project tracking and team coordination.
  • Do not store classified or third-party confidential information that you are not authorised to share with internal teammates.
